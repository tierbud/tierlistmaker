What you can actually do with it

The board embedded above is the same maker that lives on TierBud's homepage. It's not a stripped-down demo, it's the real thing.

🖱️

Drag, drop, done

Upload your own images and pull them straight into a tier. No setup screen, no tutorial to click through first. It's ready the moment the page loads.

🎨

A real design editor

Hit Create or click any item and you get a small MS Paint style toolkit: pen, shapes, emoji stamps, eraser, undo/redo, and a text tool with bold, italic, and alignment. You can even draw directly on an uploaded photo or stack extra images on top as movable layers.

🧱

Tiers built your way

Add or remove rows, rename them, set a background color and a label color for each one, and drag them into whatever order you want. You're not stuck with S through F.

🎬

Rank videos too

Paste a link from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, or nearly anywhere else and it turns into a draggable card. Click it and the video plays right there in a popup.

One click to finish. Once every item in the pool has a tier, a completion popup pops up automatically offering to download your board as an image or generate a shareable link. You don't have to hunt for a save button.

Ways people actually use it

Because the board is this flexible, it ends up getting used for more than the obvious stuff.

🎮

Game rosters and character lists

Fighting game casts, RPG party members, Pokémon generations, anything with a big list of entries works well here, especially with the AI import if you don't want to upload every image by hand.

🍔

Food, shows, and everyday debates

Fast food chains, sitcoms, songs off an album, snack brands. Anything a group of friends argues about over text is a decent candidate for a tier list.

🙈

Blind ranking for reaction content

Templates with blind mode show items one at a time, in random order, with no going back once you've placed one. It's a good fit for streamers and anyone filming a reaction-style ranking.

🧩

Custom boards from scratch

Skip templates entirely and build something with your own images and the design editor. Useful for anything niche enough that nobody's made a template for it yet.

Sharing what you build

The generated link isn't just a static picture of your board. It opens as a real page with a download button, social share buttons for places like Facebook, X, Reddit, and WhatsApp, emoji reactions so other people can react to your picks, an embed code if you want to drop the interactive version into your own post, and a comment box underneath it. Before you generate the link you can add a title and, if you want, a creator name, social handle, and photo that show up on the page.

If you'd rather keep it on the site itself, there's also a "Save as post" option after you edit a template, which walks you through a short title and category form and publishes your version as its own page, credited to you, no account required either way.
